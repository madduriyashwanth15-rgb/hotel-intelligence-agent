# Fire Emergency and Evacuation Procedure

## Code Name

A fire or smoke emergency is announced as Code Red, followed by the location. Example:
"Code Red, Valley Table kitchen".

## Discovering a Fire: RACE

- Rescue: move anyone in immediate danger away from the fire, if it is safe to do so.
- Alarm: activate the nearest manual call point and dial 5555 to report Code Red with the
  exact location.
- Contain: close doors and windows to contain smoke and fire.
- Extinguish or Evacuate: use a fire extinguisher only on a small fire and only if trained;
  otherwise evacuate.

## Using a Fire Extinguisher: PASS

Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep from side to side.

## Evacuation

- Never use the lifts during a fire. Use the marked staircases.
- The main assembly point is the front lawn near the main gate.
- Villa guests and villa staff assemble at the villa garden gate.
- Housekeeping and front office staff check corridors and assist guests with reduced
  mobility. Guests in accessible rooms 101 and 102 are assisted first.

## Roles

The Duty Manager is the incident commander until the fire brigade arrives. The front office
prints the in-house guest list and the staff attendance list for the headcount at the
assembly point. Security opens the main gate for fire engines and stops all vehicles from
entering.

## Calling External Services

The Duty Manager or the telephone operator calls the fire brigade on 101 as soon as a fire
is confirmed. Do not wait to confirm the size of the fire before calling.

## Returning to the Building

Nobody re-enters the building until the fire brigade officer in charge declares it safe and
the Duty Manager gives the all-clear.
