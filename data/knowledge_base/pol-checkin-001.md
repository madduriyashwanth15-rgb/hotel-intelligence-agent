# Check-in, Check-out, Early Arrival and Late Departure Policy

## Standard Times

Check-in time is 14:00 and check-out time is 11:00 for all room types, including suites and
villas.

## Identification Requirements

Every adult guest must present a valid government-issued photo identity document at
check-in. Foreign nationals must present their passport and a valid visa. Details of foreign
nationals must be registered with the local authorities within 24 hours of arrival, as
required by law (Form C). The front desk keeps a copy of the identity document on file.

## Payment Guarantee at Check-in

At check-in the front desk takes a pre-authorisation for the full room charges plus an
incidental deposit of ₹5,000 per room per stay. Guests on prepaid rates provide only the
incidental deposit.

## Early Check-in

Early check-in from 10:00 is free of charge, subject to availability. A guaranteed early
check-in before 10:00 must be requested in advance and is charged at 50 percent of the
nightly room rate. Guests arriving before their room is ready may store luggage at the bell
desk and use the Tea Lounge and pool.

## Late Check-out

Late check-out until 13:00 is complimentary for Gold and Platinum loyalty members, subject
to availability. For other guests, late check-out until 13:00 costs ₹1,500. Late check-out
until 18:00 is charged at 50 percent of the nightly room rate. Departures after 18:00 are
charged a full additional night.

## Express Check-out

Guests may use express check-out by leaving their key card in the express box at the front
desk. The final folio is emailed to the address on the reservation within 2 hours.

## Minimum Age

The main guest on a reservation must be at least 18 years old.
