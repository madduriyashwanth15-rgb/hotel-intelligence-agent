# Cancellation, No-Show and Refund Policy

This policy applies to all individual reservations at Grand Hills Resort. Group bookings of
five or more rooms are governed by their group contract instead.

## Flexible Rate (direct and travel agent bookings)

Guests may cancel free of charge until 48 hours before the scheduled arrival time of 14:00
on the arrival date. A cancellation made less than 48 hours before arrival is charged one
night's room rate including taxes. The remaining nights are released and not charged.

## Peak Season

Peak season runs from 1 April to 30 June and from 20 December to 5 January. During peak
season the free cancellation window is 7 days before arrival. Cancellations made within
7 days of arrival are charged 50 percent of the total room charges for the stay.

## Private Pool Villas

Private Pool Villa reservations can be cancelled free of charge until 14 days before
arrival in all seasons. Cancellations within 14 days of arrival are charged two nights at
the booked rate, or the full stay if the stay is shorter than two nights.

## Corporate Rate

Bookings made under a corporate agreement may be cancelled free of charge until 24 hours
before arrival. Later cancellations are charged one night.

## Non-Refundable Advance Purchase Rate

The advance purchase rate is fully prepaid and non-refundable. Dates cannot be changed.
Only the General Manager may approve an exception, for example a documented medical
emergency, and any exception is issued as a credit for a future stay rather than a cash
refund.

## No-Shows

A guest who does not arrive and has not cancelled by 23:59 on the arrival date is a
no-show. The first night is charged and all remaining nights are cancelled automatically.
Guests who expect to arrive after midnight must inform the front desk to keep the room.

## Bookings Made Through Online Travel Agents

Reservations made through an online travel agent (OTA) follow the cancellation terms shown
on the OTA at the time of booking. Cancellations and refunds for these bookings must be
processed through the OTA, not directly by the hotel.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 business days.
Cash payments are refunded by bank transfer after the guest provides account details.
Front office staff must record the reason for every refund in the reservation notes.

## Waivers and Exceptions

The Duty Manager may waive a cancellation charge of up to one night. Any waiver above one
night requires approval from the General Manager. Waivers must be recorded with the
approver's name.
