# In-Room Dining Menu and Service Standards

## Service Hours

In-room dining is available 24 hours a day. The full menu is served from 06:30 to 23:00. A
limited night menu is served from 23:00 to 06:30. Guests order by dialling 7 from the room
telephone.

## Delivery Time Standards

Orders must be delivered within 30 minutes for the full menu and within 20 minutes for
beverages only. If an order will be later than promised, the order taker must call the
guest with an updated time.

## Charges

A tray charge of ₹200 applies to every in-room dining order. Prices on the menu exclude
taxes. All orders are charged to the room folio and the guest signs the bill on delivery.

- Club sandwich with fries: ₹650
- Vegetable noodles: ₹550
- Masala omelette with toast: ₹450
- Cookies with hot chocolate: ₹350

## Children's Menu

Mini pasta with tomato sauce ₹350, chicken nuggets with fries ₹400 and fruit bowl ₹250, all
available during full menu hours.

## Tray Collection

Trays and dishes are collected within 45 minutes of delivery or when the guest calls. Staff
must never enter a room displaying a Do Not Disturb sign to collect trays.
