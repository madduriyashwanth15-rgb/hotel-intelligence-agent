# Medical Emergency Procedure

## Code Name

A medical emergency is announced as Code Blue, followed by the location.

## First Response

1. Stay with the guest or colleague and keep them calm.
2. Dial 5555 and report Code Blue, the exact location and the person's condition.
3. Do not move the person unless they are in immediate danger.
4. Do not give any medication, food or drink.
5. Send a colleague to the main entrance to guide the first aider or ambulance.

## First Aiders and Equipment

At least one trained first aider is on duty on every shift. Automated external
defibrillators (AEDs) are kept at the front desk, at the spa reception and at the entrance
of Valley Table. First aid kits are kept at the front desk, the kitchen, housekeeping
control and the pool.

## Ambulance and Hospital

The Duty Manager decides whether to call an ambulance on 108. The nearest hospital with an
emergency department is Hillcrest District Hospital, 14 kilometres away, about 30 minutes by
road. The resort car can be used for non-critical transfers when a staff member accompanies
the guest.

## After the Incident

The Duty Manager completes an incident report within 12 hours. Guest health information is
confidential and must only be shared with people who need it to provide care.
