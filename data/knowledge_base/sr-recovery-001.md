# Service Recovery Policy

## Principle

Every complaint is an opportunity to keep a guest. The member of staff who receives a
complaint owns it until it is resolved or formally handed over to a manager.

## The LEARN Approach

- Listen to the guest without interrupting.
- Empathise and acknowledge how the guest feels.
- Apologise sincerely, without blaming colleagues or other departments.
- React by fixing the problem quickly and telling the guest what will happen and when.
- Notify the Duty Manager and record the complaint in the guest incident log.

## Compensation Authority Levels

- Any staff member may offer a goodwill gesture worth up to ₹2,000, such as a complimentary
  drink, dessert or laundry item.
- A supervisor may approve compensation up to ₹5,000.
- The Duty Manager may approve compensation up to the value of one room night, including a
  complimentary night or room upgrade.
- Anything above one room night requires approval from the General Manager.

## Recording and Follow-up

Every complaint must be recorded in the guest incident log within 24 hours, including the
action taken and any compensation given. A manager follows up with the guest before
departure to confirm the guest is satisfied.

## Serious Complaints

Complaints involving safety, security, discrimination, injury or food poisoning are escalated
to the Duty Manager immediately. Staff must not offer compensation or admit liability for
these complaints; the Duty Manager handles them.

## Room Moves

If a room problem cannot be fixed within 30 minutes, offer the guest a room move to an
available room of the same or a higher category, at no extra charge.
