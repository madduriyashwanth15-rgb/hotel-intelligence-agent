# Housekeeping Room Cleaning and Inspection Standards

## Room Status Definitions

- Dirty: the room needs cleaning.
- Clean: the room has been cleaned by a room attendant but not yet inspected.
- Inspected: a supervisor has inspected the room and it is ready to sell.
- Out of order: the room cannot be sold because of a maintenance issue.

Only inspected rooms should be assigned to arriving guests. A room is set to out of order
only together with a maintenance task, and the front office must be informed immediately.

## Cleaning Time Standards

- Departure clean, standard rooms: 45 minutes.
- Departure clean, Hillside Suites: 60 minutes.
- Departure clean, Private Pool Villas: 90 minutes, including the plunge pool check.
- Stayover service: 25 minutes.

## Priorities

Rooms with a same-day arrival are cleaned first, followed by VIP rooms, then other
departures, then stayovers.

## Do Not Disturb

Rooms showing Do Not Disturb are not entered. If the sign is still displayed at 15:00, the
housekeeping supervisor calls the room to offer service. If there is no answer, the
supervisor and a security officer check the room together for guest safety.

## Inspection Checklist

The supervisor checks that the bed is made to standard, the bathroom is dry and stocked,
amenities are complete, the minibar is replenished, the air conditioning works and no items
from the previous guest remain.

## Turndown Service

Turndown service is provided between 18:00 and 21:00 for Hillside Suites and Private Pool
Villas, and for any guest on request.
