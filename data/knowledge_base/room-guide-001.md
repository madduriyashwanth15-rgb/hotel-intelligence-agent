# Room Types and Amenities Guide

Grand Hills Resort has 80 rooms: 76 rooms on floors 1 to 4 of the main building and four
Private Pool Villas (V01 to V04) in the villa garden. All rates below are rack rates in
Indian rupees (INR) per room per night before taxes; seasonal and channel rates vary.

## Standard King (STD-K)

24 rooms. 28 square metres with one king bed and a garden view. Maximum occupancy 2 guests.
Rack rate ₹6,500. Rooms 101 and 102 are accessible rooms with a roll-in shower, grab rails
and a lowered wardrobe rail.

## Standard Twin (STD-T)

16 rooms. 28 square metres with two single beds and a garden view. Maximum occupancy
2 guests. Rack rate ₹6,500.

## Deluxe Valley View (DLX)

20 rooms. 36 square metres with a king bed, a sofa and a private balcony overlooking the
valley. Maximum occupancy 3 guests. Rack rate ₹9,000.

## Premier Garden Room (PRM)

10 rooms. 40 square metres with direct access to a private sit-out facing the gardens.
Maximum occupancy 3 guests. Rack rate ₹12,500.

## Hillside Suite (STE)

6 suites. 65 square metres with a separate living room, a bathtub and panoramic hill views.
Maximum occupancy 4 guests. Rack rate ₹21,000. Suites include evening turndown service and
complimentary airport or railway station transfer one way.

## Private Pool Villa (VIL)

4 villas. 110 square metres, standalone, with a heated plunge pool, an outdoor shower and a
private garden. Maximum occupancy 4 guests. Rack rate ₹38,000. Villas include breakfast in
the villa or at Valley Table, evening turndown service and a dedicated butler from 07:00
to 23:00.

## Amenities in Every Room

Free high-speed Wi-Fi, a 43-inch smart television, an electric kettle with tea and coffee,
two complimentary bottles of water per day, an in-room safe, a hair dryer, an iron on
request and a minibar.

## Extra Beds and Children

An extra bed costs ₹2,000 per night and is available only in Deluxe Valley View, Premier
Garden Room, Hillside Suite and Private Pool Villa categories, within the maximum occupancy.
Children under 6 years stay free when using existing bedding. Baby cots are free on request.

## Minibar Prices

Soft drinks ₹150, juices ₹180, local craft beer ₹450, snacks from ₹120. Minibar items are
replenished daily and charged to the room folio.
