# Restaurants, Bars and Opening Hours

## Valley Table (all-day dining)

Open daily from 06:30 to 23:00 on the ground floor of the main building. The breakfast buffet
is served from 07:00 to 10:30. For guests whose rate does not include breakfast, the
breakfast buffet costs ₹1,200 per adult and ₹600 per child aged 6 to 12; children under 6
eat free. Lunch is à la carte from 12:30 to 15:30 and dinner from 19:00 to 23:00.

## Ember Grill (specialty dinner)

Open for dinner from 19:00 to 23:00, closed on Mondays. Ember Grill serves wood-fired grills
and regional hill cuisine. Reservations are recommended and can be made through the front
desk or the concierge. Smart casual dress is requested.

## Cloud Nine Bar

Open daily from 16:00 to 00:00, with last orders at 23:30. Guests must be 25 years or older
to be served alcohol, in line with local regulations. Live acoustic music is played on
Friday and Saturday evenings from 20:00.

## Tea Lounge

Open daily from 10:00 to 18:00 in the lobby. Serves estate teas, coffee, cakes and light
snacks. High tea is served from 16:00 to 18:00 for ₹950 per person.

## Breakfast Inclusion

Breakfast is included for guests in Private Pool Villas and for any reservation booked on a
bed-and-breakfast rate. The front desk confirms breakfast inclusion on the key card wallet.

## Outside Food

Outside food and beverages are not permitted in the restaurants and bar. Birthday cakes
brought by guests may be served at Valley Table for a cake-cutting fee of ₹500.
