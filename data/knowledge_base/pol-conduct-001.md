# Guest Conduct, Smoking, Pets, Visitors and Pool Rules

## Smoking

All rooms, villas and indoor areas are non-smoking. Smoking is allowed only in the
designated smoking area next to the guest car park. A cleaning fee of ₹10,000 is charged
when there is evidence of smoking in a room or villa.

## Pets

Pets are not allowed at the resort. Trained service animals assisting guests with
disabilities are welcome; please inform the front desk before arrival.

## Visitors

Visitors are welcome in public areas. Visitors to guest rooms must register at the front
desk with photo identification and must leave by 22:00.

## Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00. Music and loud noise on balconies, sit-outs and in villa
gardens are not permitted during quiet hours.

## Swimming Pool

The main pool is open from 07:00 to 20:00. There is no lifeguard on duty; guests swim at
their own risk. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Glassware is
not allowed in the pool area.

## Damage

Guests are responsible for damage to resort property caused during their stay. Charges are
assessed by the Duty Manager and added to the room folio.
