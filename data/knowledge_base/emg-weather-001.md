# Power Outage, Severe Weather and Landslide Procedure

## Power Outage

Backup generators start automatically. Emergency lighting, lifts, the kitchen and the front
desk systems are restored within 15 seconds. Power to all guest rooms is restored within
2 minutes. If power is not restored within 2 minutes, engineering checks the generator and
the front desk calls affected guests. A guest trapped in a lift is reassured over the lift
intercom while engineering performs the manual release; never attempt to force lift doors.

## Monsoon Season

The monsoon season runs from July to September. During this period guided treks and the
sunrise viewpoint drive are suspended whenever the regional weather service issues an
orange or red alert. The front desk informs arriving guests about road conditions.

## Landslide or Road Closure

If the access road is closed by a landslide, the Duty Manager informs all in-house guests,
offers to extend stays at the same rate for guests who cannot depart, and waives late
check-out charges for affected guests. Arriving guests are contacted to reschedule; no
cancellation charge applies when the road is officially closed.

## Thunderstorms

During thunderstorms the pool and outdoor areas are closed. Staff move guests indoors and
switch off outdoor music equipment.
