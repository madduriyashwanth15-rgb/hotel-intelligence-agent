# Food Allergy and Dietary Requirements SOP

## Purpose

To make sure guests with food allergies, intolerances or dietary requirements are served
safely in every outlet and through in-room dining.

## Taking the Order

1. Ask every guest whether anyone at the table has a food allergy or dietary requirement.
2. Write the allergy clearly on the order ticket and mark the ticket with the red ALLERGY
   stamp.
3. Inform the chef in person; do not rely on the ticket alone.
4. Never promise that a dish is completely free of an allergen. Tell the guest that the
   kitchen handles common allergens such as nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs, shellfish and soy.

## In the Kitchen

The chef prepares allergy orders on the separate purple chopping board with clean utensils.
The dish is plated separately and carried to the guest by the same server who took the
order. The server confirms the allergy with the guest when the dish is served.

## Vegetarian, Vegan and Jain Meals

All outlets offer vegetarian and vegan options. Jain meals, prepared without onion, garlic
or root vegetables, are available with two hours' notice.

## Allergic Reaction

If a guest shows signs of an allergic reaction, such as swelling of the lips or face,
difficulty breathing or a rash, call Code Blue immediately by dialling 5555 and follow the
Medical Emergency Procedure. Do not give the guest any medication.

## Recording

Allergies disclosed by staying guests are added to the guest profile so that every outlet
is informed for the rest of the stay.
