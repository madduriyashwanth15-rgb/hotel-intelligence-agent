# Front Office Guest Arrival SOP

## Before Arrival

The night before arrival, the front office reviews all arrivals, pre-assigns rooms and checks
special requests, loyalty tier and allergies. Platinum members and suite and villa guests are
flagged as VIP arrivals.

## Greeting

Greet every arriving guest within 30 seconds of reaching the desk, make eye contact and use
the guest's name as soon as it is known. Offer a welcome drink of chilled lemongrass tea.

## Registration Steps

1. Confirm the reservation details: name, dates, room type and rate.
2. Collect identity documents as required by the Check-in Policy.
3. Take the payment pre-authorisation and incidental deposit.
4. Issue two key cards per room unless the guest requests a different number.
5. Explain breakfast times, the Wi-Fi network and the location of the room.
6. Offer luggage assistance from the bell desk.

## VIP Arrivals

VIP guests are escorted to their room by a front office supervisor. A welcome amenity of
fresh fruit and a handwritten note is placed in the room before arrival. Villa guests are
met by their butler.

## Room Not Ready

If the room is not yet ready, apologise, store the luggage, offer the Tea Lounge and give the
guest an estimated ready time. Housekeeping is asked to prioritise the room. Call the guest
as soon as the room is ready.

## Walk-in Guests

Walk-in guests without a reservation are offered the best available rate for the room type.
Full payment for the first night is taken in advance.
