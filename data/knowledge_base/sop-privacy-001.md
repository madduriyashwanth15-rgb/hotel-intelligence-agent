# Guest Privacy and Data Protection SOP

## Room Numbers

Never say a guest's room number aloud at the front desk or over the telephone. Write it on
the key card wallet instead. Never give a guest's room number to a caller or visitor.

## Telephone Enquiries

Do not confirm whether a person is staying at the resort. Offer to transfer the call to the
room or take a message without confirming that the guest is in-house.

## Verifying Identity Before Sharing Booking Details

Before discussing or changing a reservation, verify the caller by asking for the
confirmation code and the guest's last name, plus either the arrival date or the email
address on the booking. If the caller cannot be verified, do not share any details.

## Identity Documents and Payment Cards

Copies of identity documents are stored only in the property management system, never on
paper at the desk or on personal devices. Staff must never write down full payment card
numbers.

## Use of Guest Data

Guest data is used only to provide the stay and for purposes the guest has agreed to. Guest
lists must never be shared outside the resort. Suspected data breaches are reported to the
General Manager immediately.
