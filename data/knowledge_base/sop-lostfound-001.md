# Lost and Found SOP

## Logging Found Items

Every found item is handed to housekeeping control and logged in the lost and found register
with the date, time, exact location, a description and the name of the finder. Items are
placed in a sealed bag labelled with the register number.

## Valuables

Valuables such as jewellery, cash, passports, phones and laptops are moved to the security
safe within one hour of being found. Two staff members sign for the transfer.

## Retention Periods

- Valuables are kept for 180 days.
- Other items are kept for 90 days.
- Perishable food and opened toiletries are discarded on the same day.

Items not claimed after the retention period are donated to a local charity or disposed of,
and the register is updated.

## Returning Items

Items are released only after the owner proves identity and describes the item accurately.
If the guest has already departed, the item can be shipped by courier at the guest's cost
after the guest confirms the address and payment in writing.

## Guest Enquiries

Staff must not confirm or deny over the telephone whether an item has been found until the
caller has been verified as the registered guest.
